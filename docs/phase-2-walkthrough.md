### What is actually in that file, row by row

Every line below was **measured** by putting the real file through the real endpoint

| line | the row | what happens | |

* **Line 4 — a company's nationality is dropped, not refused.** Sending a nationality on a company to

  "unvalidatable" list, so `not-an-email` lands in the customer record. The single-customer route
  *does* validate it. The import is the one way a malformed address gets into the book.

# WHAT YOU WILL NOT SEE — settled deferrals, not new defects

Three things are deliberately not done. If you find them, they are already known.

### Two typed identifiers remain on `/audit-trail`, and no picker can fix either

Both are **required**, so that screen still cannot be used without pasting a uuid.

* **`wfEntityId` is polymorphic.** It names *any* of fifteen different kinds of record, so there is no
  single picker that could serve it. Closing it needs either a "what kind of thing?" selector in front
  of a picker, or a search that spans every workflow-carrying record — a design decision about what an
  audit reader is being offered, not a mechanical fix.
* **`documentId` has no search route at all.** There is no "find a document by name" read anywhere in
  the system; `GET /documents` lists one policy's documents and nothing else. The nearest path is to
  find the policy (which now has a picker) and then its documents — a different flow, not the same one
  with a better field.

Both are in `README.md` § Known gaps with what closing each would need.

### 23 optional filters still take a raw identifier — your decision, not an oversight

Across 14 screens, mostly the dashboards. They are **optional**: the screen works without them, you
simply cannot narrow it without a uuid. Phase 2 fixed the 9 **required** ones — the fields that made a
screen unusable — and left these by your own decision.

The sources they need now all exist, so each is a small mechanical change rather than design work.
Re-measure rather than quoting the number:


### Sanctions screening is still name-only, against no real list

Unchanged by Phase 2. A customer is screened on their **name alone** — no identity number is passed to

**No screen claims otherwise**, and that was checked as its own question: `/screening-matches` carries
